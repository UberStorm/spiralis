# SPIRALIS

*From one cell to a thousand stars.*

**[Play SPIRALIS in your browser!](https://uberstorm.github.io/spiralis/)**

A six-stage evolution web game. Every creature, world, galaxy and sound is procedurally generated at runtime; surface detail comes from photorealistic CC0 PBR maps.

---

## The Stages

| Stage | You are | You do | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cell** | One organism in a tide pool | Eat, grow, dodge bigger cells, collect DNA | Outgrow the pool (3 generations) |
| **Creature** | One animal on an island | Befriend or eliminate rival species | Brain growth completes |
| **Tribal** | A village | Gather food, make tools & instruments, win over tribes | Ally or destroy all 4 tribes |
| **Civilisation** | A nation | Build land, sea, and air vehicles, claim spice & cities | Control all 7 cities |
| **Space** | An empire | Explore 200 systems, scan, terraform, colonise | Establish 3 colonies and reach the Core |

Between stages, evolve your creature in the **Creature Editor**: graft mouths, eyes, limbs, wings, and armour onto a body you reshape yourself.

---

## Controls

* **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move
* **Click & Drag**: Rotate camera
* **Mouse Wheel**: Zoom camera
* **Shift**: Sprint
* **1–8**: Abilities (attacks & social actions)
* **Space**: Take off / flap wings (requires wings)
* **Creature Stage**: Click target creature · **R** at nest to evolve
* **Tribal / Civ Stage**: Click units to select, **Tab** selects all, click terrain to issue orders
* **Space Stage**: **E** enter system · **F** scan · **T** terraform · **C** colonise · **Q** exit · **Space** fire
* **Esc**: Pause / Menu · **M**: Mute · **H**: Controls help
